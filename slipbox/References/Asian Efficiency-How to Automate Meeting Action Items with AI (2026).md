# How to Automate Meeting Action Items with AI (2026)

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-09
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/task-management/automate-meeting-action-items/

## TL;DR

The fastest way to turn meeting notes into completed tasks is a **three-layer pipeline**: Granola (or Otter) captures the meeting and extracts action items, Zapier routes each item into your task manager, and Todoist (or Notion) holds the tasks where you'll actually see them. Full pipeline runs **~$39/month**. Manual work afterward: 60-second review per meeting.

## Quick verdict

- **Todoist at $5/mo** = simplest task-manager layer for most individuals.
- **Notion** works better for teams who want meeting notes and tasks in one workspace.
- Skip Zapier if you have fewer than 2 meetings/day.

## The pipeline

After every meeting, automatically:

1. **Granola captures** the audio and generates a summary with action items.
2. **Zapier detects** the new summary and extracts each action item.
3. **Each action item** gets created as a task in Todoist or Notion (with due date + assignee).
4. **Follow-up email** gets drafted in your inbox.

Manual effort during meeting: zero. After meeting: 60 seconds of review.

## Layer 1 — Capture (Granola)

A tool that reliably **extracts action items from meetings** — not just transcribes, actually identifies "who needs to do what by when."

**Granola** ($14/user/mo, Business — no separate individual tier): records from Mac audio, **no bot joins the meeting**. Jot quick keywords during the call ("Sarah: send proposal by Friday", "pricing decision needed"). Hit "Enhance Notes" after the call → structured output with labeled action items. Granola separates action items from discussion — gives a "## Action Items" section with specific tasks, owners, deadlines.

**Otter** ($8.33/mo annual): same extraction feature. Adds a bot to your meeting. Has a feature for drafting follow-up emails directly.

Cost: $8–14/mo.

## Layer 2 — Automation (Zapier)

This is where most people skip — **and where the magic happens**. Without it: finish meeting → open Granola → read action items → copy each one to task manager → draft follow-up email. 10–15 min/meeting. With 5 meetings/day: over an hour/day on post-meeting admin.

**Zapier** ($19.99/mo for Professional). Build the workflow once:

- **Trigger**: New meeting summary in Granola (webhook or email forward).
- **Step 1**: Extract action items using Zapier's AI step. Ask it to return each as: task description, assignee, due date.
- **Step 2**: For each action item, create a task in Todoist/Notion/Asana.
- **Step 3**: Draft a follow-up email with meeting summary + action items, drop in Gmail drafts.

Case study: a clinic's weekly patient review meeting generated action items that got delegated verbally. After pipeline, every item tracked, assigned, due-dated, follow-up sent.

## Layer 3 — Task storage

- **Todoist** ($5/mo): personal, simple, fast. AI features improving but not required.
- **Notion** (free + paid): teams who want meeting notes + tasks in one workspace.

For most solo: Todoist. For teams: Notion.

## Total cost

- **Solo pipeline**: Granola $14 + Zapier $19.99 + Todoist $5 = **~$39/mo**.
- **Skip Zapier if** fewer than 2 meetings/day — manual copy isn't worth the Zapier fee.
- **Full team**: ~$80/mo with Notion Business + Zapier + Otter for everyone.

## What you'll build

| Stage | What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Smallest version of workflow | Easier to finish and maintain |
| Setup | Only the settings you need | Reduces friction and overbuilding |
| Run | After real use | Keeps the system useful |

AE's emphasis: **start with the smallest version that's actually useful**. The setup is meant to disappear into the background; if it requires babysitting every meeting, you've overbuilt.
