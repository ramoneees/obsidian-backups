# Your First AI Automation: A 15-Minute Zapier Setup

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/first-ai-automation-guide/

## TL;DR

Pick **one** simple automation that solves a real daily annoyance, build it in 15 minutes on Zapier's free plan, and run it for two weeks before adding anything else. The best first automation for most people: **star an email in Gmail → Todoist task**. Two steps, zero cost, impossible to break.

## The 3 best first automations

| Automation | When to use it | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| Starred email → Todoist task | You already star emails to "remember" them later | Gmail, Zapier, Todoist |
| Form submission → welcome email | Lead magnet, waitlist, contact form | Tally/Typeform/Google Forms, Zapier, Gmail |
| Calendly meeting ends → follow-up task | Lots of 1:1 calls | Calendly (paid), Zapier, Todoist |

## Why most people never start

The automation fantasy: email auto-sorts, tasks appear, calendar books itself, you show up with nothing on the plate. **The trap is starting with the whole system.** The right move is starting with the **most frequent annoyance** — anything you do manually 3+ times a week that feels like copy-paste.

For most people that is email-related: an email arrives, you create a task elsewhere, or you star it "to remember" and forget it.

## Step-by-step: Gmail → Todoist automation

**What you need:** Gmail (free), Todoist (free), Zapier (free — this is a 2-step Zap within the free plan).

**Step 1 — Create Zapier account.** Use your Google account to speed up the Gmail connection.

**Step 2 — Create a new Zap.** Click **Create → Zaps**. Optional: try Zapier Copilot, type "When I star an email in Gmail, create a task in Todoist" and let it scaffold.

**Step 3 — Set up the trigger (Gmail).** Search for Gmail. Choose **New Labeled Email** or **New Starred Email** (Zapier may show it as "New Labeled Email"; pick the "Starred" label — same data model in Gmail). Connect Google account, test trigger. If no starred emails exist, star one and re-test.

**Step 4 — Set up the action (Todoist).** Search Todoist. Choose **Create Task**. Map the email **Subject** field to the Task **Title**. Map **Sender** to **Description** for context. Connect Todoist, test.

**Step 5 — Turn on the Zap.** Now starring an email in Gmail creates a Todoist task automatically.

## Why the "one-at-a-time" rule matters

> Five automations started = five things to monitor. One automation started = one thing to learn whether it actually works in your life.

The right time to add a second automation is when you've used the first one enough to **notice it working** (or not). Two weeks is the minimum benchmark.
