# Quoting Tarn Adams

## Fonte

- **Source**: Simon Willison (quotation post)
- **Author**: Simon Willison
- **Published**: 2026-09-01
- **URL**: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/1/tarn-adams/

## Resumo

Quote post. Tarn Adams, cocriador do Dwarf Fortress, num tom amargo sobre o momento da indústria de games (matéria da PC Gamer: "the industry's in shambles over AI and layoff-happy CEOs — everyone I know, their bosses are slowly getting psychosis"):

> "They took the letters from me! I have to talk about *dwarf behavior* now. I can't even talk about dwarf AI. It doesn't exist. It's *dwarf behavior*, and they misbehave sometimes"

Contexto: antes da onda de IA, falar de "AI" em games era prestigioso; agora o termo ficou tão carregado/corporativo que Adams ironiza ter de renomear sua simulação de "dwarf AI" para "dwarf behavior" para não soar como hype de LLM.
