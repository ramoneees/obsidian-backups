# Superhuman vs. Spark (2026): Which Email App Wins?

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-13
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/email-management/superhuman-vs-spark/

## TL;DR

**Superhuman** wins on raw speed and **Auto Drafts** if you send 50+ emails a day. Nothing else processes email that fast. **Spark** wins on price (~$5/mo vs $33–40/mo) and cross-platform support, since Superhuman only works with Gmail and Outlook. **Important context**: Grammarly acquired Superhuman in mid-2025 and rebranded the parent company in October, but **pricing itself didn't change** because of the deal.

## Comparison snapshot

|  | Superhuman | Spark |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | No | Yes (1 account, Smart Inbox) |
| Entry paid | $30/mo (Starter) | ~$5/mo (annual) |
| Full AI features | $33/mo (Business, annual) | ~$5/mo (annual) |
| Monthly billing | $40/mo | ~$7.99/mo |
| Annual savings | ~17% | ~37% |
| Works with | Gmail, Outlook only | Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Exchange, IMAP |
| Team/collab | No | Yes (shared inbox, comments, delegation) |

## Where Superhuman wins

**Raw speed.** Nothing else is close. Keyboard-driven from the ground up. Loading threads, switching inboxes, searching — instant. Users consistently report **2–3x faster email processing**.

**Auto Drafts.** The feature that makes Superhuman worth the price for the right person. You open a thread needing a reply; **a draft is already there, written in your voice**, based on conversation context. You didn't ask for it. You didn't prompt it. It just appeared. Review, tweak one line, send. Trained on your full email history. Learns how you write.

**Auto Labels triage.** Every incoming email sorted before you open the app: needs response, waiting on someone, meetings, cold pitches. Triage is already done.

**Keyboard shortcuts mastery.** Investment required. But once learned, you're never reaching for the mouse.

## Where Spark wins

**Price.** Free tier does things no free email client has any business doing — Smart Inbox sorts into Personal/Newsletters/Notifications automatically. Premium $5/mo adds AI Compose, thread summarization, and "My Writing Style" feature that learns your voice. **6x cheaper than Superhuman's entry plan.** For someone sending 20 emails a day, Spark Premium does 80% of what matters.

**Cross-platform.** Spark's strongest card. Mac, iOS, Android, Windows, web — all polished, native. Superhuman is Gmail/Outlook only. If you're on iPhone + Windows laptop, or have Google + iCloud accounts, or your team uses Exchange plus personal Gmail — Spark.

**The Gatekeeper feature.** When someone emails you for the first time, Spark holds it in a separate queue. You approve or block. One click. After that, permanent: in or out. Different take on inbox management — **less about processing faster, more about controlling what you process at all**.

**Shared inbox.** Comments on threads, delegation to teammates, internal notes. Critical for client-facing teams.

## The Grammarly-acquired Superhuman question

Mid-2025: Grammarly acquired Superhuman. October 2025: parent rebrand. **Pricing didn't change** because of the deal — the original article's framing on this was wrong (corrected here).

What did change: long-term product direction. Expect Superhuman to integrate more deeply with Grammarly's writing assistant and grammar-check stack. If your AI drafting already happens through a different layer, this matters less.

## The deciding factor

**Daily email volume.** That's it.

- < 30 emails/day → Spark Premium wins on cost
- 30–50/day → wash, pick by ecosystem (Apple/Win → Spark, Gmail-only → either)
- 50+/day → Superhuman's Auto Drafts pays for itself in cognitive load removed, not literal minutes
