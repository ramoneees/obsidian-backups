# Why Your AI Workflow Isn't Getting Used (And the Fix Is Simpler Than You Think)

**Thanh Pham** — diagnosis of why AI workflow deployments fail. The most common failure mode: the workflow works, but people don't use it. The fix is rarely "more features" or "better training" — it's matching the workflow to where the user already is.

## Core idea
- Adoption failure is rarely an AI capability problem
- It's usually an integration problem — the workflow doesn't fit the user's actual day
- The fix is to start with the user's existing routines and build the AI workflow into them, not the other way around
