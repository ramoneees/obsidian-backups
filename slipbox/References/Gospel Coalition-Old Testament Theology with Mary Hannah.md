## Episode Summary (from page description)

Matt Harmon and Ben Gladd talk with **Mary Hannah** about:

- Old Testament theology
- Biblical theology in the local church
- Christ-centered interpretation
- Moral application
- How ordinary Christians can grow in seeing the beauty of Christ across Scripture

## Guest

**Mary Hannah** (PhD, Trinity Evangelical Divinity School) — director of women in ministry at [Second Presbyterian Church](https://www.2pc.org/) in Memphis, Tennessee. Also teaches Old Testament part-time at [Memphis City Seminary](https://memphiscityseminary.org/).

## Hosts

- **Benjamin L. Gladd** (PhD, Wheaton) — TGC executive director of The Carson Center for Theological Renewal. Previously professor of New Testament at Reformed Theological Seminary (12 years). Author of *From Adam and Israel to the Church*, *Handbook on the Gospels*, *The Story Retold*.
- **Matthew Harmon** (PhD, Wheaton) — professor of New Testament at Grace College and Theological Seminary. Author of commentaries on Galatians, Philippians, 2 Peter, Jude. Books on biblical theology including *Rebels and Exiles* and *The Servant of the Lord and His Servant People*.
