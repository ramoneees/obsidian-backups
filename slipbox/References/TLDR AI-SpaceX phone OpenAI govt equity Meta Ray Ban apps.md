# TLDR 2026-07-02

**Lead stories**
- **OpenAI proposes 5% stake to Trump administration to ease Washington pressure** (4 min, CNBC) — ~$42.6B stake. Sam Altman: best way to share AI upside is giving the public a financial interest. Larger proposal: US gov 5% of each leading AI dev.
- **SpaceX Showed Investors Prototype of Elon Musk's New AI Device** (5 min, WSJ) — Handset-like device, proprietary OS, Qualcomm Snapdragon, integrates SpaceX AI. Seen prior to SpaceX IPO. Could reduce Musk's reliance on Apple.
- **Rise of the Cheap Robots** (4 min) — Several sub-$10K general-purpose robots. Nori Robotics bimanual <$1,400. BracketBot wheeled <$3,000. Weave's Isaac 1 home mobile manipulator for $8,000 or $450/month.
- **For the First Time, a Cell Built From Scratch Grows and Divides** (13 min, Quanta) — Lab-made cell with nonliving components grew, replicated DNA, divided. Not alive (no survival without external food/ribosomes), but strongest demonstration yet that life can be built from nonlife.
- **Meta Build for Wearables** (18 min) — Meta Ray-Ban Display Web Apps use standard web APIs. Easiest path: AI coding tools (Replit, Claude Code, Cursor).
- **ZCode** — Official IDE for GLM-5.2. macOS/Windows/Linux. GLM Coding Plan subscribers get 1.5x usage quota.

**Other notable items**
- **The Anthropic Fable Ban Is Over. The Battle Over How to Tame AI Has Just Begun** (5 min, WSJ) — Debate over federal control of cutting-edge API tools is heating up. Tension between safety regulation and "stay ahead of China."
- **Meta Is Planning a Cloud Business to Sell AI Computing Power** (6 min, Bloomberg) — Selling access to excess AI compute. AWS Bedrock-style model hosting or raw compute (à la CoreWeave).
- **Most AI Work Can Wait** (3 min) — Design systems around routing, not models.
- **Google Reader was building the wrong future** (11 min) — It was the social network that people wanted all along.
