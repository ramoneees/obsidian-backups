# The Subtraction Strategy: Why Removing Friction Beats Adding Goals Every Time

**Thanh Pham** — TPS 618 podcast episode. The "subtraction" thesis: in most workflows, the next big productivity gain comes from **removing friction**, not adding new goals. Goals add to your list; friction-removal shrinks the list.

## Core idea
- Most productivity advice is additive: add this habit, add this system, add this goal
- But most stalled workflows are bottlenecked by friction (something that requires manual cleanup, waiting, switching tools, re-entering data)
- Subtracting one piece of friction can do the work of adding five new habits
