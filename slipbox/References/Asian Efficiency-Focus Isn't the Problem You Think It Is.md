# Focus Isn't the Problem You Think It Is

**Thanh Pham** — contrarian take on focus. The conventional wisdom is that modern workers are scattered and need to protect their focus. Thanh's diagnosis: for most knowledge workers in 2026, "focus" is no longer the binding constraint. What is?

## Core idea
- Modern AI agents can run a lot of the focused work
- The bottleneck is shifting to *taste* — knowing which work to do
- "I can't focus for 4 hours" is a smaller problem than "I spent 4 focused hours on the wrong thing"
