# How to Automate Your Weekly Review with AI

**Thanh Pham** — applying AI to the canonical GTD weekly review. The weekly review is the keystone GTD habit; automating it (or parts of it) means the system can run more often and with less friction.

## Core idea
- GTD's weekly review is the highest-leverage habit, but it's also the one most often skipped
- AI can pre-stage: collect open loops, surface unprocessed inputs, summarize the week
- Human review time shrinks from ~60 minutes to ~15 minutes; consistency goes up
