# How to Automate Your Weekly Review with AI

## The TL;DR

Best way to start: **ChatGPT as voice-based review coach** + your calendar + open projects in front of you → AI turns the conversation into a clean weekly summary. Cuts the review to ~15 min. Add Notion / Zapier / agent layer later.

## Why It Matters

The weekly review is the single most important productivity habit the author teaches — and the one most people skip. Manual: 30–45 min of sitting with a checklist. People skip. Monday arrives reactive.

AI doesn't replace the review. It makes it 15 min and actually enjoyable.

**The old way (what the author did for 15 years)**: Evernote template, manual calendar walkthrough, project updates, typed wins/losses/lessons, typed priorities.

**The AI way**: Open ChatGPT voice mode → "be my weekly productivity coach, ask these questions one at a time" → ramble through answers while looking at calendar → ChatGPT summarizes and generates weekly plan → paste into Notion.

## The 5 Questions

1. **Get Clear**: What loose ends are floating around?
2. **Get Current**: What's on my calendar? Active projects? What's stuck?
3. **Get Creative**: Opportunities or risks to think about for next week?
4. **Filter**: What can I eliminate or say no to?
5. **Commit**: Top 3 priorities for next week — put them on the calendar.

## Setup Steps

### Step 1: AI Review Coach (5 min)

Custom prompt — act as weekly productivity coach, ask these questions one at a time:

1. What were your biggest wins this week?
2. What didn't go as planned?
3. What's still open or unfinished?
4. Looking at next week's calendar, what needs preparation?
5. What are the three most important things to accomplish next week?
6. Anything you should say no to or cancel?

### Step 2: Automate Data Gathering (10 min, one-time)

- **Option A (Simple, free)**: Keep calendar open while talking to ChatGPT. Works fine.
- **Option B (Zapier)**: Sunday-morning Zap pulls last week + next week calendar + incomplete tasks from Notion → email/Slack summary.
- **Option C (Lindy agent)**: Fully automated Sunday review brief pulling from calendar, email, Notion, task system.

### Step 3: Do the Review (15 min, weekly)

Author's actual Sunday routine:
- 5 min — Open ChatGPT voice mode, start the review conversation
- 5 min — ChatGPT asks follow-up questions ("You mentioned the workshop didn't fill up... what would you do differently?")
- 5 min — Generate summary, edit, paste into Notion weekly review page

**Total: 15 min. Used to be 45.**

### Step 4: Feed Results Into Your System

- Top 3 priorities → block time on calendar
- Open issues → update Notion Projects DB
- Things to cancel/decline → send those emails/messages NOW
- Weekly summary → save in Notion second brain

### Step 5: Use Claude for Deep Thinking (optional)

Paste review summary into Claude, ask:
- "What pattern do you see in what's not getting done?"
- "I keep saying this project is important but never work on it. Why?"
- "Here's next week's calendar. Where should I block deep work?"

Claude pushes back in ways ChatGPT doesn't — useful for breaking stuck patterns.

## Pro Tips

- Do it at the same time every week
- Voice mode trick is real — talking processes differently than typing
- Don't skip "what to cancel" — highest-leverage question every week
- Keep it short: 15–20 min is enough. If 45 min, you're overthinking it.
- Review your reviews quarterly — patterns emerge about which goals keep appearing without progress
