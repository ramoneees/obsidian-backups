## Core thesis (from the lab's framing)

> **Grace is God's favor toward us, his power for us, and his gifts to us.**

Piper unpacks 1 Corinthians 1:4–9 in three movements, each defining a facet of grace:
1. **Favor** — God delights in his people (vv. 4–5, "I give thanks… for the grace of God given to you in Christ Jesus")
2. **Power** — God strengthens them "to the end" (vv. 7–8, "so that you are not lacking in any spiritual gift… He will sustain you to the end")
3. **Gifts** — the spiritual gifts themselves are grace-vehicles (vv. 5, 7)
