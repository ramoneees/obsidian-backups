# The 20-Hour Weekly Briefing That Now Takes 15 Minutes

**Thanh Pham** — another concrete case study of the "reactive → proactive AI" transformation. The team used to spend ~20 hours/week assembling a briefing for the next week. After agentifying it, the same output takes 15 minutes of human review.

## Core idea
- Most recurring "research" tasks are decomposable into structured research + pattern recognition
- Agents do the structured research faster, more thoroughly, and more consistently
- Human value shifts to deciding what to do with the briefing, not assembling it
