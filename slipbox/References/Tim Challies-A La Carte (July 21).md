## Recommended Reading (curated by Tim)

### 1. [The Preacher's Life](https://www.covenantseminary.edu/theology/the-preachers-life) — H.B. Charles, Jr.
Looks to 2 Timothy 2:15 ("approved by God") and explains how he's considered it devotionally. Three questions: Is God well pleased with me? Is my work well done? Is the Word rightly handled? Commended to preachers.

### 2. [A Life of Prayer](https://joelellis.substack.com/p/a-life-of-prayer) — Joel Ellis
> "Many evangelical Christians think about their 'prayer life' in terms of devotional habits and practices: morning Bible reading, an inspiring and theologically superficial devotional, and a prayer list, but we want to encourage believers at RPC to develop the discipline of a 'life of prayer.'"

The semantic shift is intentional: a prayer life is devotional; a life of prayer is whole-life orientation.

### 3. [Conforming to Non-Conformity](https://www.caseymccall.com/p/conforming-to-non-conformity) — Casey McCall
How non-conformity can quickly become its own form of conformity (Nirvana-shirt phenomenon). Christians are called to reject conformity to the world and pursue transformation through the renewal of our minds in Christ (Rom 12:1–2), but some efforts to resist conformity are disguised versions of it.

### 4. [The Miracle of Christian Unity](https://michaeljensen603.substack.com/p/the-miracle-of-christian-unity) — Michael Jensen
Calls Christians to remember and enact the miracle of Christian unity. The day-after-day barrage of back-and-forth argument between Christians is "very wearing." It can make you think Christians don't agree about anything at all — but "can the faith they proclaim be true, really?"

### 5. [5 Signs Your Marriage Is Unhealthy (And What to Do About It)](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/article/5-signs-marriage-unhealthy/) — Curtis & Jenny Solomon
TGC piece on warning signs of an unhealthy marriage. If one spouse thinks there's a problem in one or more of these areas, there is. Reach out for help — pastor, small-group leader, or another person in leadership in your local church.

### 6. [Football Won't Save the World](https://murraycampbell.substack.com/p/football-wont-save-the-world) — Murray Campbell
Reflections after the World Cup. "One hears of World Cups from yesteryear when there was Maradona or when Pelé controlled the pitch. Older generations salivate at the skill and competition yet I suspect we have just enjoyed the best ever World Cup."
