# 10 AI Automations That Save Knowledge Workers 10+ Hours a Week

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-10
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/ai-automations-10-hours/

## TL;DR

Ten small automations stacked together recover roughly **10–13 hours/week** for knowledge workers. Not because any single one is dramatic — each replaces a repetitive task with an AI-drafted first pass you review. **Start with email drafting** (highest frequency, lowest risk, ~20 min setup). Meeting notes and inbox triage are the next two highest-leverage. The real payoff is running several at once so they feed each other clean data.

## How AE evaluated

Each automation judged on: (a) does it actually remove work rather than just move it around, (b) does a human still review before anything goes out externally, (c) how much setup time to get real value. Prices re-verified July 2026 — Lindy in particular overhauled its plans since this piece first ran.

## Three highest-leverage starting points

### 1. Meeting notes + action items (Granola + Lindy)

Granola runs locally on Mac and captures every meeting without a bot joining. After the call, a Lindy agent pulls the transcript, extracts action items, drafts a follow-up email in your voice. **Manual effort: ~60 sec/meeting — review draft and send.** Was 15–20 min/meeting; back-to-back days this compounds fast. One tax firm owner during tax season deployed two similar agents and went from 2–4 hrs/day on meeting prep/follow-up to almost zero.

### 2. Email drafting (Lindy)

Lindy monitors the inbox, **drafts replies in your voice**, queues for one-click approval. 5–10 min saved per email × ~15 emails/day = the math. Bigger thing: **the cognitive switch from authoring to editing**. Approve when 80% right; edit when needed; delete and write fresh <10% of the time.

> "The real magic isn't in the sending. It's in the drafting." — AE to a skeptical client, December 2025. The client set it up and hasn't gone back.

### 3. Inbox triage (Lindy + SaneBox)

Combines Lindy's AI triage with SaneBox's smart folders. Whitelist known senders, flag cold pitches for one-click review, route newsletters to a low-priority lane.

## Two mid-tier additions

### 4. Meeting scheduling (Lindy)

CC a Lindy scheduler instead of proposing times manually. It checks constraints (calls only 1–5 PM, max three/day, no back-to-back), replies with available slots, confirms the pick. Setup tip: most failures come from vaguely defined constraints up front — clients who tried "be reasonable" got unusable results; explicit constraints worked first try.

### 5. Voice-to-task capture (Whisper Flow + Todoist)

Talk to your phone while walking, get Todoist tasks queued for triage. ~30–45 min/week recovered, mostly for people who commute or walk a lot.

## The compounding insight

Each automation alone is incremental. **Stacked together**: meeting notes → action items → todo → Friday planning brief → daily dispatch. The pipeline effect is where the 10–13 hours/week comes from — not any single tool.

AE's pattern: "I'd rather have ten 80%-right drafts to edit than zero from-scratch drafts to send." The point is the **cognitive unload**, not the literal time.
