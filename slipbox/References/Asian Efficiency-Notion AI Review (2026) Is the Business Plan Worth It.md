# Notion AI Review (2026): Is the Business Plan Worth It?

**Source**: Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date**: 2026-07-21
**URL**: https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/notion-ai-review/

## TL;DR

Notion AI's standout feature is AI search across the entire workspace — alone worth the Business plan for large workspaces. It is a convenient generalist (lives where your data lives), not a specialist: weaker than Claude for writing, weaker than Granola for meeting notes. Upgrade if you already use Notion for team collaboration; skip if you have a solid Claude + ChatGPT stack.

## Key Points

- **AI Search (the killer feature)**: Ask natural-language questions across the entire workspace — pages, databases, meeting notes, comments. "What did we decide about the Q2 launch?" returns a synthesized answer from your actual data.
- **AI Writing**: Summarize, draft, translate, tone-adjust, fix grammar, generate action items from meeting notes — works inside Notion, no context switching.
- **AI Agents (new in 2025–2026)**: Analyze comments, calendar events, Slack messages, GitHub repos; surface patterns and take actions based on user-defined rules.
- **Custom AI Skills + Workers (March 2026 update)**: Reusable AI commands + code-executing "Workers" — Notion is moving from "AI assistant" to "AI automation platform."
- **Meeting Notes AI**: Custom instructions per meeting; auto-summary, action items, follow-ups. Not as specialized as Granola.
- **AI Connectors**: Search across Google Drive, Slack, and other integrations through Notion AI — searching your entire work ecosystem, not just Notion.

### What works
- **Lives where your data lives** — no copy-paste workflow.
- **AI search across everything** — "what's the status of the Medici project?" returns instant answer from actual data.
- **Includes the trajectory** — every quarter brings meaningful new capabilities (worth betting on).
- **Included in Business tier** — no separate expense if already on Business.

### What doesn't
- **Limited on Free/Plus** — full AI requires Business ($20/mo); Plus ($10/mo) only gives a taste.
- **Not a specialist** — beaten by Claude (writing), Granola (meeting notes), Perplexity (search) in their lanes.
- **AI Search isn't real-time** — it's still indexed over Notion, not over Slack/Drive live (Connectors help but not perfectly).

## Verdict

**Upgrade if**: You're already on Notion for team collaboration and have a workspace of 100+ pages.
**Skip if**: You have a solid Claude + ChatGPT stack and Notion is just a personal wiki.
