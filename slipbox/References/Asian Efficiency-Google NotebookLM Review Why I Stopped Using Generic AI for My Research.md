# Google NotebookLM Review: Why I Stopped Using Generic AI for My Research

**Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)** — NotebookLM is Google's research-focused AI: upload your sources (PDFs, Google Docs, websites), and it answers grounded in only those sources. AE's thesis: for *research* specifically (not just chat), grounded AI tied to a specific corpus is materially better than general-purpose chat AI.

## Core idea
- Generic AI: trained on the internet, hallucinates, no source discipline
- NotebookLM: grounded in your sources; cites; refuses to answer beyond the corpus
- For research, the second pattern is what you want
