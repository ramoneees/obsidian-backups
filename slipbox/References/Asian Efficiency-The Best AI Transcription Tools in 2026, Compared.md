# The Best AI Transcription Tools in 2026, Compared

## Quick Recommendations

| Tool | Best For | Pricing | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Otter.ai** | Meetings | $8–17/mo | Auto-joins Zoom/Teams/Meet, real-time transcription, AI summaries |
| **Descript** | Podcasts & video editing | $16–50/mo | Transcript-as-editor (delete text = delete audio) |
| **Rev** | Occasional use | $0.25–1.99/min | Pay-per-use, no subscription |
| **AssemblyAI** | Developers | $0.15/hr | API for product/agent integration |
| **Whisper** | Free local | Free | Open-source, runs locally, privacy |

## Detailed Picks

### 1. Otter.ai — Best for Meetings

- Auto-joins scheduled meetings without setup
- Real-time transcription during calls
- AI summaries with action items
- Searchable archive
- Free tier: 300 min/month (~5h)
- Pro $8–17/mo (1,200 min), Business $20–30/mo (6,000 min)
- **Downsides**: visible bot awkward in client meetings; 90-min conversation limit on Pro; accuracy drops with accents/jargon
- **Note**: Author switched to Granola for client meetings because of the visible bot — Otter excellent for internal meetings

### 2. Descript — Best for Content Creators

- Transcript-based editing: delete sentence → audio cuts
- Studio Sound fixes bad audio in one click
- Overdub voice cloning for corrections
- AI Actions repurpose content automatically
- ~95% accuracy on clear English
- Free 1h / Hobbyist $16/mo annual (10h) / Creator $24/mo annual (10h) / Business $50/mo annual (30h)
- Used by AE for The Productivity Show: 2–3h edit → 45min

### 3. Rev — Best Pay-Per-Use

- $0.25/min AI, $1.99/min human
- No subscription, no minimum
- Fast turnaround (AI instant, human hours)
- **Best for**: journalists, researchers, occasional transcription, human-accuracy needs

### 4. AssemblyAI — Best for Developers

- API only — send audio, get back transcripts + summaries + speaker labels + topic detection + entity recognition
- $50 free credit to start, $0.15/hr base
- Several of AE's Lindy agents use it behind the scenes
- **For**: AI agent builders, automation engineers

### 5. OpenAI Whisper — Best Free Option

- Completely free, runs locally (privacy)
- 99 languages supported
- Surprisingly accurate, even with accents
- **Catch**: command-line or third-party app required — not beginner-friendly

## Notes e Conexões

- The author uses Otter for internal meetings but Granola for client meetings (bot problem)
- Pricing re-verified July 2026 — several plans changed
- For podcast/video editing, Descript replaces two tools (transcription + editor)
- AI transcription hours are plan-limited on every paid service
