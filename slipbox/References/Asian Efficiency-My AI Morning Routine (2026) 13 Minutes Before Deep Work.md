# My AI Morning Routine (2026): 13 Minutes Before Deep Work

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/productivity/ai-morning-routine/

## TL;DR

A 5-tool sequence runs in 13 minutes before deep work starts: **Brain.fm → Superhuman → Lindy briefing agent → Claude → Notion**. Total cost ≈ $126/month with Lindy; budget version runs **$14/month** (Brain.fm + ChatGPT Go + Notion free). The order matters — each tool sets up the next one. Reversing it turns a proactive morning reactive.

## Quick verdict

- The order matters: audio → email awareness → briefing → planning → execution (proactive before reactive).
- Total setup time ≈ 13 minutes; the rest is real work.
- Budget version: Brain.fm + ChatGPT Go + Notion free = **$14/month**, skips the Lindy briefing agent.

## The morning sequence

| Time | Tool | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 6:30 | Brain.fm | Focus audio starts, brain transitions from sleep to work mode |
| 6:45 | Superhuman | 5-min inbox scan — no responding, just awareness |
| 7:00 | Lindy agent | Morning briefing on every meeting: who, background, open items |
| 7:15 | Claude | 5-min session planning the deep work block into timed steps |
| 7:30 | Notion | Deep work begins — everything before this was preparation |

## 6:30 — Brain.fm ($6/mo annual)

Functional AI-generated audio designed for focus, not music. Plays through headphones while planning. "Focus" mode during morning work; "Sleep" mode at night.

**Why it's in the routine:** Transition from "just woke up" to "brain is online." Zero effort — hit play and work.

## 6:45 — Superhuman inbox scan ($30/mo)

5-minute inbox scan with **no responding**. Superhuman's split inbox shows VIPs, clients, and time-sensitive items first, separate from noise. AI drafts wait for after deep work.

**The rule:** Scan but don't respond before the first focus block. The scan takes 5 minutes; responding takes an hour.

**Why:** Reduces inbox anxiety without pulling you into email mode.

## 7:00 — Lindy morning briefing agent (part of Lindy Max $199.99/mo)

Agent runs while you sleep and delivers at 7am a briefing covering every meeting:

- Who you're meeting with and their role
- Brief background (LinkedIn + past interactions)
- What you discussed last time (meeting notes history)
- Open action items between you
- Profile link for quick reference

Reads in ~3 minutes. Replaces 10–15 minutes of pre-call googling and email-scanning.

## 7:15 — Claude planning session ($20/mo Pro)

Open Claude and run a morning planning conversation. Describe priorities; Claude breaks work into timed steps. Removes the "staring at blank screen" problem before deep work begins.

## 7:30 — Deep work starts in Notion

Everything before this was preparation. The session in Notion captures the plan and starts executing.

## Key principles

- **Proactive before reactive.** Audio first, then awareness, then planning, then execution.
- **Scan ≠ respond.** Knowing what's in the inbox is awareness; responding is a different activity for later.
- **Each tool sets up the next.** Reversing the order breaks the system.
- **Cost scales with autonomy.** Lindy (autonomous agent) drives the high cost; budget version uses ChatGPT Go instead.

## Notes

- The Lindy briefing agent is the most expensive piece. Skip it on a budget — Claude can substitute for the planning step, and the email scan in Superhuman covers most awareness needs.
- Brain.fm's value is the *transition* effect, not the focus effect. The author noticed a real difference in the first week.
- A morning routine optimized for 13 minutes of overhead is essentially "pay $126/mo to protect your mornings from entropy."
