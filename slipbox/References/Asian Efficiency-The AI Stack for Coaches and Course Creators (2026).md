# The AI Stack for Coaches and Course Creators (2026)

## TL;DR

Stack para coaches e course creators organizada por **fase do workflow**: Create (Claude $20 + Descript $24), Package (Teachable $39-189 + Circle $89), Coach (Granola $14 + Notion $20), Market (ChatGPT $8-20 + Canva $10 + Zapier $0-20). **Starter total: $93/mês**. **Established total: ~$286/mês** — uma venda de curso a $197 cobre dois meses.

## Resumo

### Workflow Phase 1: Create Content

- **Claude Pro** ($20/mês): outlines de curso, scripts de módulos, frameworks, sales pages. **Ponto importante**: Claude puxa de volta em design curricular fraco ("Módulo 3 assume conhecimento que você não ensinou no Módulo 2"). É o diferencial vs. ChatGPT para design de curso.
- **Descript** ($24/mês): edição de vídeo por transcript. Studio Sound, Overdub, Eye Contact AI. Aula de 3h vira 45min.

### Workflow Phase 2: Package & Deliver

- **Teachable** ($39-189/mês): AI Course Starter gera currículo, Quiz Generator cria assessments, transcrição com tradução 70+ idiomas.
- **Circle** ($89/mês): comunidade. AI agents para FAQs e onboarding. Por que separado: course completion rates pulam com comunidade. Facebook Group não serve — você não controla.

### Workflow Phase 3: Coach

- **Granola** ($14/mês): notas de coaching calls sem bot na call. Summary + action items.
- **Notion** ($20/mês): página por cliente (session notes, action items, progress). Cohort tracking.

### Workflow Phase 4: Market & Sell

- **ChatGPT** ($8-20/mês): posts sociais, ad copy, market research de objeções.
- **Canva** ($10/mês): thumbnails, social graphics, lead magnets.
- **Zapier** ($0-20/mês): automação — enrollment → welcome sequence, completion → community invite.

### Course Creation Workflow (Idea → Launch)

1. Validate via ChatGPT (research demand, test positioning)
2. Outline via Claude (curriculum structure)
3. Script via Claude (talking points, examples, exercises)
4. Record via Descript
5. Build via Teachable (upload, AI quizzes, payment)
6. Design via Canva (thumbnail, marketing)
7. Launch via Zapier (enrollment automation)
8. Support via Circle + Notion

**Antes da AI**: 6-8 semanas. **Com AI**: 2-3 semanas focused work.

## Pricing Breakdown

### Starter coach/creator ($93/mês)

Claude Pro $20 + Descript Hobbyist $12 + Teachable Starter $39 + Notion Free + ChatGPT Go $8 + Canva Free + Zapier Free + Granola $14. Skip Circle no início.

Claude Pro $20 + Descript Creator $24 + Teachable Builder $69 (annual) + Circle Professional $89 + Notion Business $20 + ChatGPT Plus $20 + Canva Pro $10 + Zapier Professional $20 + Granola $14.

Curso a $197 = dois meses da stack established. Cliente de coaching a $500/mês = stack inteira com lucro.
