# Review: 'The Path to Being a Missionary' by Ryan Robertson and Matt Bennett

**Source:** The Gospel Coalition (book review)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**URL:** https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/reviews/path-being-missionary/

## Book at a glance

- **Title:** _The Path to Being a Missionary: A Guide for the Aspiring_
- **Authors:** Ryan Robertson (president, Reaching & Teaching) + Matt Bennett (professor of missions and theology, Cedarville University)
- **Publisher:** Crossway, 208 pp.
- **Reviewed:** as "the best book I've encountered for aspiring missionaries"

## Core frame: not "Am I called?" — "Am I qualified?"

The book reframes the standard missionary-discernment question. **Instead of "Am I called?"** — which often functions as a vague emotional signal — **Robertson and Bennett urge "Am I qualified?"** The book is "a launch pad into conversations" between church leaders and candidates about **character, conviction, and competency**.

## Missionary as a Mature Disciple

> "If man's lostness is our primary motivation, then our methods will inevitably be changed. We must all be motivated by what God is motivated by—his glory."

- Missions **doesn't begin with desire for adventure** or willingness to sacrifice — it begins with **a grand vision of God's glory**.
- Sound doctrine matters: "investigate the early creeds and confessions of faith" (81). _"The first job of a missionary is to speak about God, and God has spoken first about himself"_ (87).
- Missions doesn't end with a new convert or scattered sheep — it ends with **disciples gathered into local churches**. **The local church is the means and ends of missions**; missionaries need a healthy ecclesiology.
- _"The most important thing about any particular church is not what makes it unique from other churches but what it has in common with every true church throughout time"_ (98).
- **Central:** being a functional member of a local church _before_ going. _"Does your local church observe today the same gifts you seek to use overseas in the future?"_ (38). **No plane ride is long enough to transform a reluctant evangelist into a bold preacher or a shallow Christian into a mature disciple maker.**

## Missionary as a Wise Traveler

Many aspiring missionaries major in zeal. They minor on wisdom.

- Eager to see a new church planted but **may not themselves be ready to plant**.
- Ready to share the gospel with those who've never heard **but may not understand the culture well enough to be clear**.
- Often **don't even know what questions they should be asking**.

## Why this review resonated (reviewer's testimony)

The reviewer and his wife moved to the Middle East a decade ago after a long discernment process pieced together from various (mostly unhelpful) resources. Looking back, _The Path to Being a Missionary_ would have been the **trusted guide they wished they'd had**. Robertson's academic + missions-organization experience combined with Bennett's academic + pastoral experience is the right combination for the question "Am I qualified?"
