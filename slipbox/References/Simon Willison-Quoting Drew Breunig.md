# Quoting Drew Breunig

**Fonte**: Simon Willison — citação de Drew Breunig
**Data**: 2026-08-23 · **URL**: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/23/drew-breunig/

## Resumo

Simon guarda a citação do post de Drew Breunig, [*Fable & The End of the Free Lunch*](https://www.dbreunig.com/2026/08/23/fable-the-end-of-moore-s-law.html):

> "Prior to Fable, it felt silly to waste *too* much time improving your coding harness or context strategies. A new model would arrive at the same price (or cheaper!) and paper over most of your problems.
>
> But then Fable landed. It was (and still is!) *incredible*. But the cost was so high and Opus was *good enough* (as was 5.6, K3, and even GLM) for *most* of the code we needed.
>
> *So we started to think about what work went where.*"

## Notas e Conexões

- Tese: a era em que cada geração de modelos "papelava" os problemas de harness/roteamento acabou — com Fable caríssimo no topo e modelos *good enough* baratos embaixo, otimizar **onde** cada trabalho corre virou alavanca econômica primeira.
- Conecta diretamente com a stack local de modelos (pesado→cloud, privado→local): [[Stratechery-Fable 5, Anthropic Alignment, AI Tiers]] e [[Simon Willison-Claude Fable is relentlessly proactive]].
- Ecoa [[resumo-better-models-worse-tools]] — a qualidade do modelo não substitui a qualidade do fluxo/harness.
- "End of the free lunch" = paralelo com o fim da Lei de Moore: ganhos futuros vêm de engenharia, não de upgrade automático.
