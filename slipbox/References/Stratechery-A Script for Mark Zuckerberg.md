# A Script for Mark Zuckerberg

**Ben Thompson** — Stratechery. A creative piece: the script Thompson thinks Zuckerberg *should* deliver at Meta's Q2 2026 earnings call, in early August. (Gated beyond the preview unless subscriber.) Captured the headline below.

## The setup
> "The setting: Meta's earnings call in early August, 2026. The speaker: Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg."

> "I know it's weird that I, Mark Zuckerberg, am doing the Director of Investor Relations job, but anything is possible when this speech is made up. What follows isn't actually me: it's what Ben Thompson of Stratechery thinks I should say on this call."

## Three "mistakes" Thompson would have Zuckerberg confess
1. **Mobile platform obsession** — Facebook Games, payments, etc.; "Apple saved us from my mistaken obsession. Mobile Made Facebook Just an App, and that was Great News."
2. **Reality Labs / VR** — "I invested twelve figures into this technology because I thought it was cool, and yes, because I wanted to own a platform." 12-figure losses.
3. **Failure to appreciate the ad business** — Took the connection → ad business for granted while obsessed with platform ownership. TikTok figured out entertainment-first before Meta did.

> "What I've come to realize is that all of these mistakes are symptoms of what has been my biggest failing as CEO: all of you on this call have appreciated our ad business more than I have. I've been very blessed as CEO to have excellent co-workers who have over the years developed the world's best digital ad business, while I frankly haven't taken as much interest as I should have."

> "Building a platform is antithetical to building an ad business. A platform's goal is to feature third-parties; an advertiser's goal is to capture attention for itself."

## Why AI matters (the punchline)
> "First, when investors compliment our asset-light business, what they are complimenting is the fact that our business is purely digital. Everything digital, however, is firmly within AI's cross-hairs. It may seem odd to begin my AI pitch by highlighting terminal value risk, but today is about honesty: every single digital company on earth faces an existential threat from AI, and we are no exception."

Then Thompson would have Zuckerberg commit to **selling excess compute on the spot market** (à la the broader big-tech "renting out excess compute" trend referenced in [[TLDR AI-Meta AI images inside XBox layoffs tech worker sentiment]]):

> "Making our compute available for rent means that we can only take it back if we can make more money on it ourselves; the only way we can do that is by leaning into what we are good at, not what I have spent too long wanting us to be."

> "We are not out here to make chatbots or compete with OpenAI and Anthropic; they can fight for work and productivity and charging subscriptions and replacing humans. Our goal is to celebrate humans, to connect them, to entertain them, and to enable commerce among them."
