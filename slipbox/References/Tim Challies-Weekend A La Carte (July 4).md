# Weekend A La Carte (July 4)

Saturday edition themed around America's 250th anniversary.

## America-250 reads
- **250 Years of Faith** — Thomas Kidd at Desiring God on Christianity in America from the Founding to the present. "Our brave new world has made many Christians eager to recapture the nation's spiritual origins… the church's flourishing in America has depended not on a connection to the government but on the strength of its sovereign God."
- **The Presbyterian Rebellion** — George Grant (republished at Ligonier): Horace Walpole, "Cousin America has eloped with a Presbyterian parson." John Witherspoon, "as high a Son of Liberty, as any Man in America." See [[Ligonier-The Presbyterian Rebellion]]
- **250 Years Later: Canada, America, and the Gift of Difference** — Andrew Noble, writing Canadian, on the 250th and what it means for Canadian identity
- **Where Did Lincoln Deliver the Gettysburg Address?** — Allen C. Guelzo: yes, "he did go to Gettysburg" — and yes, there's actually some uncertainty about where exactly the platform was on Cemetery Hill
- **The Price of an Imperfect Union** — Eddie LaRow: Lincoln's "Meditation on the Divine Will" and the Emancipation Proclamation. "Two blood payments" — Antietam and the cross.
