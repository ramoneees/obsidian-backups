# Everything I Might Have Been: Trusting God's Providence

**Source:** Tim Challies
**Date:** 2026-07-13
**URL:** https://www.challies.com/articles/everything-i-might-have-been/

## TL;DR

The world feels unfair when we ponder all we might have been with different circumstances — different schools, different hobbies, different parents. But **God sovereignly places each of us exactly where we can best serve and honor him**. "Joseph's place was in the prison, Aaron's place was in the tabernacle, Esther's place was in the palace, and my place is right where he's placed me." And in heaven, we'll finally have **all of eternity** to discover what we could have been.

## The provocation

Tuition at Appleby College (just across town from Challies) creeps toward six figures. What might he have been with that caliber of tutelage? An engineer? A scientist? An English lit teacher-mentored poet? A few towns north, a highly regarded rugby club — what if his parents had enrolled him? Could hockey have unveiled latent athletic gifts? What about cooking, woodworking, an instrument?

Maybe he could have been a chef, an artisan, a virtuoso. **Maybe. If only.**

## The reframe

The problem isn't that the world is unfair. It's that our horizons are not as expansive as we wish. Our opportunities are not as broad. **We ourselves are limited.** We cannot be everything. We cannot do everything we might have done had we been different people.

> "Joseph's place was in the prison, Aaron's place was in the tabernacle, Esther's place was in the palace, and my place is right where he's placed me."

**It is good to know that God is sovereign over all of this.** He places each of us in the circumstances in which we can best thrive, best serve, best honor him. **My station, my field, my area of responsibility is exactly the one he has called me to, and I simply need to trust that he knows best.**

## The eternal consolation

> It is quite certain that part of the joy of heaven will be **discovering what I could have been**.

All of eternity to explore our gifts and develop new ones. All of eternity to enjoy our talents and foster others. **It is in heaven that I will finally know who I might have been** — and have time, opportunity, and endless ages to become it.
