# Rethinking Email, Focus & Doing Less w/ Ari Meisel (TPS621)

> **Status:** podcast landing page — no body content. Transcription not on page. The notes below are extracted from the description + cheat sheet.

## Core Idea

Ari Meisel's diagnosis: **you have ~24 good decisions per day**. Your inbox, with its thousands of tiny choices, is quietly bankrupting you before lunch.

His reframe: **email is a decision-making problem, not a communication problem.** He's replaced almost all his messaging with **asynchronous voice** (Voxer). Runs a coaching business, closes sales, stays close with far-flung friends — without a single call on his calendar. Constraints made him more effective, not less.

## Key Takeaway

Treat inbox as a **decision budget**, not a communication channel. If your day starts with 24 decisions and email burns 100, you're bankrupt by 11am. Async voice is Ari's answer — fewer decisions, more context per message, no calendar real estate consumed.
