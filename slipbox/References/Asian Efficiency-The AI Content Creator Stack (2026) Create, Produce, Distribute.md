# The AI Content Creator Stack (2026): Create, Produce, Distribute

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/ai-stack-content-creators/

## TL;DR

Content creation breaks into three phases — **create, produce, distribute** — and a different AI tool owns each. **Claude** writes, **Descript** edits audio/video, **Canva** handles design, **Zapier + Lindy** automate distribution. Three budget tiers:

- **Starter:** ChatGPT Go + Descript Hobbyist ≈ **$24/month**
- **Serious:** + Claude Pro, Opus Clip, Canva Pro, Notion Plus ≈ **$87/month**
- **Full production:** everything at top tier with Lindy ≈ **$193/month**

## The pipeline

| Phase | Tool | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Create | Claude, ChatGPT, Notion | Writing, ideation, content calendar |
| Produce | Descript, Opus Clip, Canva | Audio/video editing, short clips, graphics |
| Distribute | Zapier, Lindy | Cross-platform publishing, automation |

## Phase 1 — Create (writing + ideation)

### Claude ($20/mo) — the writing engine

Every long-form piece starts in Claude (blog posts, newsletter drafts, workshop materials, show notes). Voice profile makes first drafts sound like the author instead of generic AI.

- Blog post: ~30 min (used to be 2 hours).
- Podcast → blog post via transcript: ~15 minutes.

**Why not ChatGPT for long-form:** Claude writes more nuanced, natural content. ChatGPT is fine for short social posts (<200 words) but Claude wins for everything longer.

### ChatGPT ($0–20/mo) — quick content

Social captions, subject line brainstorming, image generation, quick research. Voice mode for ideation on walks.

**Cost-saving:** Go plan ($8/mo) covers most needs. Plus ($20/mo) only needed for DALL-E or advanced reasoning.

### Notion ($20/mo) — the content operating system

Content calendar, editorial pipeline, idea backlog, research notes, published archive — all in one workspace. Story bank system stores anecdotes and real examples so the podcast show-notes agent pulls authentic material instead of inventing generic ones.

## Phase 2 — Produce (audio + video + design)

### Descript ($16–35/mo) — the media studio

Edit the transcript; the video follows. Delete a sentence from text → audio/video disappears. No timeline scrubbing.

- Podcast workflow: record → auto-transcribe → edit transcript like a doc → export cleaned audio. 2–3 hours of editing → ~45 minutes.
- **Overdub:** voice clone from 3 min of audio.
- **Studio Sound:** fixes terrible recording quality with one click.
- **Eye Contact AI:** makes you look at camera even when reading notes.
- **AI Actions:** repurpose podcast episode into blog posts, social clips, summaries.

### Opus Clip — short-form clips

Auto-clips long-form video into short-form vertical content for TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

### Canva Pro — design

Graphics, thumbnails, social visuals.

## Phase 3 — Distribute (automation)

### Zapier + Lindy

Cross-platform publishing and automation. Lindy agents handle the orchestration that Zapier chains can't easily express.

## Notes

- The decision framework is the **phase**, not features. Don't pick a tool — pick a phase problem to solve.
- Descript is the highest-leverage tool if you produce audio/video. The transcript-as-editing metaphor is the unlock.
- The Notion **story bank** is the most under-rated piece: it makes AI-generated content feel real because the source material is real.
- ChatGPT's voice mode is genuinely useful for ideation walks; treat it as a brainstorming partner.
