## Recommended Reading (highlights)

### How Can We Be Ready to Meet the Lord? — Randy Alcorn (EPM)

Addresses the basic question. "If we are His children, God is for us, not against us. He has assured us our names are written in the Book of Life, and we won't face the Great White Throne Judgment. He wants to commend and reward us at the judgment seat of Christ. He doesn't want the works of our lifetime to go up in smoke. He wants us to have eternal rewards—and He has given us every resource in Christ to live the godly life that will result in those eternal rewards."

### Aging Is a Series of Relinquishments — Barbara Harper

A reflection on aging as increments of letting go. "I heard someone on the radio years ago say that one reason our bodies start falling apart as we age is to make us willing to let loose of them. We have such a strong survival instinct, and we often want to stay for our families. But gradually we admit that heaven looks much better than life here. God is gracious to make us aware of our mortality in increments. As we face each relinquishment, we're geared a little more toward eternity."

### Happy Birthday to You! Celebrating a Wonder of God — Ayrian Yasar

A defense of celebrating birthdays. "Why are birthdays special? As we get older, we can sometimes feel birthdays aren't as special or as important to celebrate. They just aren't as exciting as when we were kids. While the Bible doesn't command a celebration of birthdays, there are good reasons to continue to celebrate the birth of a person—and a birthday is a perfect time to do it."

### Scattered — Melissa Edgington

On the strange shift when family units disperse. "It's a strange feeling when you shift from having a tight little family unit that moves through the world together to this scattered collection of human beings who wave to one another from a distance. In some ways it feels like I'm picking up pieces of a former life I once knew and trying to make them fit into a new puzzle."

### Should You Marry Her Anyway? — John Piper (Ask Pastor John)

Pastoral counsel on marrying a spouse with significant sexual history. Two sentences the link-blogger flags as gold: **"Don't treat the transforming grace of God as less powerful than the corrupting power of sin. That would dishonor Christ, dishonor the Holy Spirit."**

### Be Sure Your Sin Will Find You Out — Tessa Lind

On the slow uncovering of hidden sin. "I wonder how often we sin and cover our sins. It may be something small. Nobody knows. Nobody will ever find out… Some people may hint at knowing our secret, but a few white lies are enough to satiate their curiosity. No one will ever know." (But it will.)

## Headlines (Christian news)

- **Released After 9 Months.** Chinese house church leader Pastor Jin "Ezra" Mingri has been released after 9 months in custody and traveled to the United States. Reunion came after sustained prayer + advocacy by daughter Grace Jin Drexel and husband Bill (Hudson Institute senior fellow).
- **Young Army Recruits.** UK military chaplains report an unusual number of young people professing faith and being baptized. "We've got a generation coming through now who are finding themselves feeling quite hopeless… The quiet revival that's being talked about, I think, is a reality and we're seeing evidence of that."
