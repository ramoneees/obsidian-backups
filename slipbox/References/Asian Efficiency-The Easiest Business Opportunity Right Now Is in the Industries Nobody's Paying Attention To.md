# The Easiest Business Opportunity Right Now Is in the Industries Nobody's Paying Attention To

**Thanh Pham** — a companion to [[Asian Efficiency-The Brad Jacobs Playbook, Updated for AI]]. The thesis: the most accessible AI-era business opportunity is to apply obvious-but-novel technology to industries that have been ignored by the tech world.

## Core idea
- Industries that haven't changed in 10+ years (local services, medical practices, etc.) are the lowest-hanging fruit
- Boring = ignored by tech talent = tech debt compounding quietly
- Modern AI can close the gap in weeks, not years
