# How to Learn Anything Faster With AI (2026)

**Source**: Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date**: 2026-07-21
**URL**: https://www.asianefficiency.com/productivity/learn-anything-faster-with-ai/

## TL;DR

Match the tool to the job: ChatGPT for general research, Claude for deep understanding and pushback, NotebookLM to learn from your own materials, Anki to make it stick. Learn by doing, not watching — have AI generate practice and critique your mistakes as you go. Every tool on this list has a free tier that's genuinely useful.

## Key Points

### Tool selection by learning task

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| ChatGPT | Quick research, overview from zero, voice-mode conversational learning | Free / $20/mo |
| Claude | Deep understanding, technical subjects, pushback on wrong mental models | Free / $20/mo |
| NotebookLM | Learning from your own books, courses, notes (grounded in your sources) | Free |
| Anki | Memorization via AI-generated spaced-repetition flashcards | Free |

### The 4-step AI learning workflow

1. **Start with a 30-minute foundation**: Use ChatGPT or Claude with the prompt:
   > "I want to learn {topic}. I'm a complete beginner. In under 500 words, give me: (1) what this is and why it matters, (2) the 5 most important concepts, (3) the most common beginner mistakes, (4) a 30-day learning path."
2. **Learn by doing, not watching**: Tell Claude what you want to build. Let it generate the code/content. Then ask it to explain every section. Modify one thing, see what breaks, ask why.
3. **Cross-check between two models** — each catches things the other misses.
4. **Make it stick with Anki** — use AI to generate flashcards, then spaced repetition handles retention.

### Application by domain
- **Coding**: Tell Claude what to build. Generate code. Explain every section. Modify one thing. See what breaks.
- **Writing**: Have Claude draft something, then critique its own draft and explain what makes each section strong/weak.
- **New industry**: Ask ChatGPT for insider terminology, key players, top three debates. Have it quiz you on what you've learned.

## Por que isso me interessa

This is the cleanest framework for "use AI to actually learn, not just skim faster" — separates the four real learning tasks (research / deep understanding / own materials / memorization) and assigns each a tool that wins its lane. Cross-checking between models is the under-appreciated move.
