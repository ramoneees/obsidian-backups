# Unhindered: The Word Unbound, The Gospel Proclaimed

*Sponsored by [The Master's Academy International (TMAI)](https://bit.ly/twp26-challies)*

## Theme
**Together We Pray 2026** — July 19–26. Theme: "Unhindered: The Word Unbound, the Gospel Proclaimed."

## Background
35 years ago, John MacArthur's invitation to meet Ukrainian pastors after the Cold War set in motion a global movement of pastoral training. Today:
- **20 TMAI training centers** across North/Central/South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Middle East
- Training **indigenous** church leaders to be faithful pastor-teachers
- **8,000+ graduates** serving worldwide, many in regions of persecution and spiritual famine

## The Anchor Text
> "...I endure hardship even to chains as a criminal. But the word of God has not been chained." — **2 Timothy 2:9 (LSB)**

Though Paul was bound, the gospel was not. Opposition cannot hinder the transforming power of God's Word. His truth always accomplishes what He purposes (**Isaiah 55:11**).

As the world denies truth, God often uses that opposition to strengthen His people and advance His gospel.

## Why Prayer
The Great Commission involves every believer. From Ukraine to Uganda, pastor or student, retiree or young adult — every Christian participates through prayer.

During campaign week (July 20–25):
- Daily prayer emails
- Video updates from ministry leaders worldwide
- Specific ways to pray for each region

## The Need
Many are spiritually starving, deceived by false teaching, weakened by shallow doctrine, or without access to sound biblical preaching. Yet wherever faithful men proclaim Scripture, people respond with hunger. They're not merely seeking sermons — they're longing for the transforming power of God's truth.
