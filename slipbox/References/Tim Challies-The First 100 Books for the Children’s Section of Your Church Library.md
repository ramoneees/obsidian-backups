# The First 100 Books for the Children’s Section of Your Church Library

**Fonte:** Tim Challies (Resources) | **Data:** 2026-11-11 | **URL:** https://www.challies.com/articles/the-first-100-books-for-the-childrens-section-of-your-church-library/

## Resumo

Sequência direta da lista dos primeiros 250 livros para biblioteca de igreja (que cobriu apenas títulos para adultos): agora são ~100 livros — mais uma lista de séries no final — para a seção infantil, organizados por faixa etária, com PDF para download e links de afiliado (Amazon e Westminster Books).

## Ideias principais

- **Board Books (0–3 anos)**, destaques: *The Beginner’s Gospel Story Bible* (Jared Kennedy); *Read-Aloud Bible Stories, Vol. 1* (Ella K. Lindvall); *Little Me, Big God* (Steph Williams); *Seek and Find: Old Testament Bible Stories* (Sarah Parker); *God Cares for Me* (Kristen Wetherell); *My First 123 Book of Bible Numbers* e *My First ABC Book of Bible Verses* (Jonathan Gibson); *Training Young Hearts* boxed set (Abbey Wedgeworth); *Found: Psalm 23* (Sally Lloyd-Jones); *The Garden, the Curtain and the Cross* em versão board (Carl Laferton)
- **Picture Books (3–7 anos)**, destaques: *The Jesus Storybook Bible* (Lloyd-Jones); *The Biggest Story Bible Storybook* (Kevin DeYoung); *The Big Picture Story Bible* (David Helm); *The Kingdom and the King Storybook Bible* (Bob Hartman); *The Gospel Story Bible* (Marty Machowski); *The Acrostic of God* (Jonathan Gibson); *C Is for Christian* (Alistair Begg); *The Big Wide Welcome Storybook* (Trillia Newbell); *God’s Great Big Global Church* (o próprio Challies); *God’s Go-Togethers* (Sam Allberry); *Jesus, Strong and Kind* (Sinclair Ferguson); *The Prince’s Poison Cup* e *The Donkey Who Carried a King* (R.C. Sproul); *Arlo and the Keep-Out Club* (Betsy Childs Howard)
- **Séries recomendadas no fim da lista**: *Wingfeather Saga* (Andrew Peterson); *The Green Ember* (S.D. Smith); *The Dream Keeper Saga* (Kathryn Butler); Lightkeepers (Irene Howat); títulos de Chris Morphew
