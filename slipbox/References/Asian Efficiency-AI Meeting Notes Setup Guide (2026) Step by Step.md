# AI Meeting Notes Setup Guide (2026): Step by Step

**Source:** Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date:** 2026-07-09
**URL:** https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/ai-meeting-notes-setup-guide/

## TL;DR

Pick the meeting note tool that fits your environment: **Granola for external or sensitive virtual calls** (no bot, local audio capture), **Otter for internal team meetings** (cheaper, bot OK), **Plaud for in-person capture** (~$159 device). Then connect notes to your task manager so action items leave the transcript. "Don't overthink this." Start with the simplest version of the workflow.

**Basics (30 min)**: one AI meeting note tool + one task manager + ~$14–20/mo.

**Full pipeline (2–3 hrs)**: basics + Zapier/Make ($20–30/mo) + Gmail drafts. Optional Plaud device for in-person (~$159 + sub). Useful CRM if sales/consulting (HubSpot, Airtable).

## Step 1 — Pick your capture tool

Two questions:

1. **Do you take meetings with external people (clients, investors, prospects)?** Get **Granola** ($14/user/mo, Business). Records from device audio, **no bot joins**. Nobody knows you're recording. "I've had clients refuse to talk openly when they saw a recording bot."
2. **Are meetings mostly internal (team syncs, standups)?** Get **Otter** ($8.33/mo annual). Sends a bot to your Zoom/Teams/Meet. Cheaper than Granola, real free tier (300 min/mo).
3. **Do you meet people in person a lot?** Add a **Plaud device** ($159 + ~$8–20/mo). Small physical recorder, clips to your shirt. Works offline, no Wi-Fi. Carry it to every conference, coffee meeting, dinner.

Pick one (or two) and move on.

## Step 2 — Set up the tool

**Granola**: download from granola.ai (Mac/Windows) → grant audio permissions → Granola auto-detects meeting and starts capturing → during call, type rough notes ("Pricing.", "Follow up with Sarah.", "Decision: go with option B.") → after call, click **"Enhance Notes"** → structured summaries with action items. **Pro tip**: rough notes guide the AI. No typing → generic summary. Typing what matters → focused summary. Spend 30 seconds flagging important stuff.

**Otter**: sign up at otter.ai → connect Google/Outlook calendar → enable **OtterPilot** → bot joins meetings automatically → real-time transcription during call → post-meeting summary with action items. **Pro tip**: check auto-join settings — by default OtterPilot joins every meeting on your calendar. Set to "ask before joining" to avoid joining meetings where you don't want a bot.

**Plaud**: charge via USB-C → download Plaud app → ... (full setup in source).
