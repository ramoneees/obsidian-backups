# How to Use Claude as a Writing Assistant (2026)

**Source**: Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date**: 2026-07-21
**URL**: https://www.asianefficiency.com/technology/claude-writing-assistant/

## TL;DR

Claude writes better than ChatGPT for anything where quality matters more than speed — proposals, articles, client emails — because it pushes back on weak ideas. Highest-ROI step (most-skipped): spend 10 minutes teaching Claude your voice. Pair with Claude Pro's Projects for persistent context. First drafts drop from 2 hours to ~30 minutes.

## Key Points

### Claude vs ChatGPT for writing
- **ChatGPT**: does exactly what you ask. "Write me a 500-word email" → 500-word email. Clean, fast, no questions.
- **Claude**: thinks about what you asked. May say "a 500-word email is too long, here's a 200-word version instead" or "your proposed structure buries the most important point." That pushback is annoying sometimes but makes output better.

For quick obedient writing → ChatGPT. For anything where quality matters → Claude.

### The 10-minute voice setup (highest-ROI step)

1. **Create a voice profile**: tone, sentence length preferences, words/phrases used often, things never done (no em dashes, no semicolons), a few examples of actual writing.
2. **Feed it at start of every conversation**: paste voice profile + "Match this style in everything you produce for me today."
3. **Use Claude Projects** (advanced): upload voice profile + writing samples + reference material. Every conversation in that project starts with Claude already knowing the style.

AE's actual voice prompt example:
> "You're writing as me. Friendly, casual voice. Short sentences. Use ellipsis (…) to extend thoughts, never em dashes. No ALL CAPS. No jargon. Use bullet points and numbered lists when explaining steps. Light humor and everyday analogies. Specific details, not generic statements. If a sentence could apply to anyone, it's too vague… make it specific to my experience."

### Five ways AE uses Claude for writing

1. **First drafts of everything** — describe audience/purpose/length/key points → let Claude draft → flag what to change → ask for revision → final polish by hand (10–15% touch).
2. **Editing & tightening** — feed your own draft and ask Claude to cut length or flag weak spots (Claude's critique often more useful than its first drafts).
3. **Brainstorming alternatives** — "give me 5 angles I haven't considered."
4. **Translation** — preserve voice across languages.
5. **Templates library** — saved prompts for recurring formats (blog posts, client emails, social posts).

### Why Pro at $20/mo is the sweet spot
- The Projects feature alone justifies the upgrade — persistent context eliminates re-pasting voice setup every session.
