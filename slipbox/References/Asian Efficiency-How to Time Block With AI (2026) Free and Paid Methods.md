# How to Time Block With AI (2026): Free and Paid Methods

**Source**: Asian Efficiency (Thanh Pham)
**Date**: 2026-07-21
**URL**: https://www.asianefficiency.com/schedule-management/time-block-with-ai/
**Last updated**: 2026-07-06

## TL;DR

You don't need a paid tool to start time blocking — a free Google Calendar + ChatGPT setup gets you most of the way there in ~15 minutes. Upgrade to Reclaim ($8/mo), Motion ($19/mo), or Sunsama ($20/mo) when you want the AI to protect and reschedule blocks automatically. Rules matter more than the tool — block your energy, protect the first block, accept some blocks will break.

## Key Points

### The free method (Google Calendar + ChatGPT, ~15 min)

1. **Identify your Big Three** (5 min): every morning, three most important outcomes. AE's 3-3-3 method = 3 major outcomes, 3 minor tasks, 3 maintenance items.
2. **Block peak hours first** (5 min): 2–3 hours in the morning (before 11am for AE). Recurring "Deep Work" or "Focus Block" event, marked Busy.
3. **Ask ChatGPT to plan your week** (5 min): "Here are my priorities for this week: {list}. I have focus time blocked from {X} to {Y} each morning. I have these meetings: {list}. Create a day-by-day plan that assigns each priority to a specific focus block. Leave buffer. Flag overcommitment."
4. **Add buffer blocks** (2 min): 15–30 min after every meeting + at end of each day.
5. **Protect the blocks** (ongoing): say no when someone tries to schedule over them; use Google Calendar's Focus Time feature to auto-decline meeting requests.

### Paid tools (premium method)

| Tool | Price | Differentiator |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Reclaim.ai** | $8/user/mo (annual) / $10 monthly | Best value; auto-blocks habits + tasks + meetings |
| **Motion** | $19/mo | Strongest auto-rescheduling; project-aware |
| **Sunsama** | $20/mo | Daily planning ritual; intent before calendar |

### Rules that stick (more important than the tool)
- Block your energy, not just your time
- Protect the first block of the day
- Add buffer after meetings (15–30 min)
- Cut planned to-do list by 30% — future self is pathologically optimistic
- Accept some blocks will break
